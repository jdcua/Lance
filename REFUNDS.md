# Lance Refund Policy

Lance Pro (monthly, yearly) and Lance Lifetime are sold with a 30-day, no-questions-asked money-back guarantee from the date of purchase.

- Reply to your Paddle receipt within 30 days for a full refund.
- After 30 days, yearly plans can be cancelled so they do not renew; completed periods are not refunded.
- Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the billed month.
- If a Pro feature is broken and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, you get a refund for the affected period even past 30 days.

Payments are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record. Refunds are issued to the original payment method through Paddle.

Free Lance is free forever. Expiring or cancelling never locks you out of your own data: your records stay on your machine and remain fully exportable.
