REFUNDS

Lance Pro (monthly, yearly) and Lance Lifetime are sold with a 30-day, no-questions-asked money-back guarantee from the date of purchase. If a Pro feature is broken and cannot be fixed within a reasonable time, the affected period is refunded even past 30 days. Payments are processed by Paddle and refunded to the original payment method. Expiring or cancelling never locks users out of their own data: records stay on the device and remain fully exportable.
