Privacy Policy

Effective 2026 · Lance is local-first. Your business data lives on your device, never in the cloud.

Your data stays home
Your business data (clients, projects, invoices, proposals, time entries, finances) lives in a SQLite database on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app’s data folder removes everything — export your records first from Settings if you want a copy.
Accessibility that adapts to you
Lance automatically detects your device’s accessibility settings (reduced motion, high contrast, text size, color scheme) and applies them locally so the app adapts to your needs. This detection happens entirely on your device — nothing is sent to any server, and we never store or transmit these settings.
What leaves your device
Update check. On launch, Lance fetches a version file from GitHub Releases. GitHub sees a normal HTTPS request (your IP, the app version in the URL). No account, no identifier, no usage data.

Changelog. The “What’s new” screen fetches public release notes from the GitHub API. Same as above: no identifier, no usage data.

License validation. Only if you enter a Pro license key: the key is checked against our license server so paid features unlock. Keyless users send nothing.

Checkout. Only if you click buy: your browser opens Paddle (our merchant of record), which handles payment, tax, and receipts under Paddle’s own privacy policy.

Contact form. If you use the contact form on this site, your name, email, and message are emailed to us so we can reply. Nothing else.

v2 waitlist (opt-in). One confirmation now and a single email when v2 ships. No newsletter, no sharing, ever. Reply “forget me” anytime and it’s deleted.

Anonymous A/B counts. This site counts anonymous views and clicks per variant. We store no IP address for these, and build no fingerprint. A 30-day cookie remembers which version of a page you saw so the page does not change on you.
What never happens
No analytics, no usage tracking, no crash reports sent anywhere.

No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no fingerprinting of your screen, hardware, or clipboard. Your business data never leaves your device.

No account system. There is nothing to sign into and no server holding your data.

Web fonts. This website and the desktop app load their typefaces from a third-party font host, which sees your IP address and browser as any web host does. That is the only third party involved. The browser trial loads no fonts from anywhere and makes no third-party request at all.

* Core free forever. No account. No cloud. Client payments go straight to you.
