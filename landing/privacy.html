Privacy Policy

Effective 2026 · Last updated October 5, 2026 · Lance is local-first. Your business data lives on your device, never in the cloud.

Your data stays home
Your business data (clients, projects, invoices, proposals, time entries, finances) lives in a SQLite database on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app’s data folder removes everything — export your records first from Settings if you want a copy.
Accessibility that adapts to you
Lance automatically detects your device’s accessibility settings (reduced motion, high contrast, text size, color scheme) and applies them locally so the app adapts to your needs. This detection happens entirely on your device — nothing is sent to any server, and we never store or transmit these settings.
What leaves your device
Update checks and downloads. The app asks our own Cloudflare worker for the latest version, and downloads run through that worker too. The worker fetches release files from GitHub with a server token, so GitHub sees the worker rather than your IP directly. Requests carry only what is needed to serve the right file, such as the app version. No account, no identifier, no usage data.

Changelog. The “What’s new” screen shows the same public release notes over the same path. Same privacy as above.

License validation. Only if you enter a Pro license key: the app sends only the key to our license server so paid features unlock. Keyless users send nothing. The server stores the key, its tier, the purchaser email we received from Paddle, the Paddle transaction and subscription IDs, the issue date, and a revoked flag used for refunds. No device IDs, ever.

Checkout. Only if you click buy: your browser opens Paddle, our merchant of record, which handles payment, tax, and receipts under Paddle’s own privacy policy.

Contact form. If you use the contact form on this site, we receive your name, email, and message by email through Resend so we can reply. Nothing else.

v2 waitlist (opt-in). Email address only, sent through Resend. One confirmation now and a single email when v2 ships. No newsletter, no sharing, ever. Reply “forget me” anytime and it’s deleted.

Bug reports. If you use the bug form, we receive your name, email, a short title, and details. Reports are stored in our private GitHub repository, labeled “user-report” so they stay easy to find, and used only to respond to you.

Anonymous A/B counts. One small experiment is running on the download button text. We count anonymous views and clicks per variant. We store no IP address for these, and build no fingerprint. A 30-day cookie (lance_ab_hero-cta) remembers which button text you saw so the page does not change on you.
What never happens
No third-party analytics, no usage tracking, no crash reports sent anywhere.

No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers in the app, no fingerprinting of your screen, hardware, or clipboard. Your business data never leaves your device.

No account system. There is nothing to sign into and no server holding your data.

Rate limiting. Our workers look at IP addresses only to slow down abuse. Those addresses live in short-lived memory and are never written to storage. Cloudflare still sees IPs because it hosts this site, as any host does.

Web fonts. This website and the desktop app ship their typefaces with the product — no font CDN, no third-party request for typography.
How long we keep messages
Contact and bug report messages are kept only as long as needed to help you and to keep a basic support history. Waitlist addresses are kept only until v2 ships, or until you ask to be removed.

To see, correct, or delete anything you sent us, email support@example.com from the same address you used, and tell us what to do. We reply to every request ourselves.
Children and changes
Lance is built for freelancers running a business. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from children.

When this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will post the new version on this page, update the last-updated date above, and note what changed. Small clarifications are dated but not announced elsewhere.

Questions about any of this: support@example.com.
