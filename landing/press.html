What it is

Lance is a desktop app (Tauri + React + SQLite + Rust) that runs a freelance business: proposals, contracts, projects, time tracking, invoices, expenses, insights. No cloud, no accounts, no telemetry.

◉
Who it's for

Independent freelancers and contractors. 41 craft profiles customize the workspace; 7 themes. Core free forever.

$
Pricing

Core $0 forever. Pro $99/yr ($14/mo), 30-day trial. Lifetime $249 one-time ($149 launch week). Client payments go straight to the freelancer; Lance never takes a cut.

◈
Privacy

SQLite stays on device. The only outbound request is the updater version check. v1 has zero AI; local on-device AI drafting ships in v2.

✎
Founder

Juan Diego Cua builds decentralized software that runs on your machine, not someone else's server. Independent developer; answers support himself.
