Why Lance

Built different. On purpose.

01
Everything talks to each other.

A client you add once flows into proposals, contracts, invoices, and insights. No re-entry. No copy-pasting. Ever.

02
Your data stays on your machine.

No accounts. No cloud sync. No server that can get breached, sold, or shut down. Your client list and financials are yours.

03
The AI sharpens your judgment.

Not replacing your instincts — supporting them. Scope warnings, pricing analysis, win rate patterns. Your edge, amplified.
