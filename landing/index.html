Why Lance
Built different.
On purpose.

Other tools sprawl across tabs and subscriptions. Lance collapses your entire operation into one fast, local, opinionated app.

01
Everything talks to each other.

A client you add once flows into proposals, contracts, invoices, and insights. No re-entry. No copy-pasting. Ever.

02
Your data stays on your machine.

Local-first by design. Works completely offline. No internet required. Your client list and financials stay on your machine, always.

03

Not replacing your instincts. Supporting them. Scope warnings, pricing analysis, win rate patterns. Your edge, amplified.
