FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT
Between [YOUR NAME] ("Contractor") and [CLIENT NAME] ("Client"), dated [DATE].

1. SERVICES
Contractor will perform: [scope, same words as the proposal].

2. PAYMENT
Fee: $[X] ([50% upfront / weekly / monthly]). Invoices due within [14] days. Late balance accrues [X]% per month. Client reimburses pre-approved expenses.

3. REVISIONS
[1] revision round included per deliverable. Further revisions at $[RATE]/hr, quoted before work starts.

4. KILL FEE
If Client cancels after work begins: 50% of remaining fee. If Contractor cancels: full refund of unearned prepayment.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Full IP transfers to Client on final payment. Contractor retains the right to show the work in a portfolio.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties keep shared business information private, during and after this agreement.

7. TERM & TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with [14] days written notice. Completed work and kill fee (if any) are payable.

8. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
Contractor is not an employee; handles own taxes, insurance, and equipment.

Signed: ______________________ ([YOUR NAME], [DATE])
Signed: ______________________ ([CLIENT NAME], [DATE])

(Template, not legal advice. For large engagements, have a lawyer review it.)
